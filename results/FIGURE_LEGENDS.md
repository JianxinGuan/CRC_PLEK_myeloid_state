# Final figure legends

## Figure 1. Cross-platform evidence architecture and frozen interpretation

The frozen platelet-related transcriptional states were evaluated sequentially using five GEO cohorts, TCGA-COAD/READ, an integrated CRC single-cell atlas, and a seven-patient Visium dataset. The evidence supports a reproducible neutrophil-related bulk association and resolves the stable component as predominantly PLEK-driven, while rejecting prognostic, platelet-specific, and established spatial-mechanistic interpretations.

## Figure 2. The frozen 13-gene platelet-related state is not associated with overall survival

Cohort-specific hazard ratios represent a one-standard-deviation increase in the frozen 13-gene state. The diamond denotes the REML random-effects estimate across five cohorts. The analysis included 1,044 patients and 398 deaths. CI, confidence interval; OS, overall survival.

## Figure 3. The frozen three-gene association is reproducible but has unequal drivers

(A) Cohort-specific Spearman correlations between the frozen PF4/PPBP/PLEK axis and the neutrophil module, with the REML estimate shown as a diamond. All five cohorts showed positive effects. (B) Random-effects estimates for the frozen score, individual genes, and leave-one-gene-out scores. PLEK was the primary driver, PPBP was a stable secondary bulk component, and PF4 showed no independent association. Error bars indicate 95% confidence intervals.

## Figure 4. Cross-platform source triangulation resolves a PLEK-dominant myeloid component

(A) Multivariable TCGA source decomposition of the frozen three-gene state after simultaneous adjustment for tumor purity and microenvironment modules. (B) Detection frequencies of PLEK, PPBP, and PF4 across selected tumor-tissue cell types in the integrated CRC single-cell atlas. Values are percentages of cells. (C) Study-adjusted donor bulk-like associations with neutrophil fraction. (D) Patient-level random-effects same-spot spatial associations. Spatial effects were small and were not specific to the neutrophil module. Error bars indicate 95% confidence intervals.

## Supplementary Figure S1. The 10-gene identity/endothelial association did not replicate

Cohort-specific and REML-pooled Spearman correlations between the frozen 10-gene identity/adhesion axis and the endothelial module. Four of five cohorts had negative estimates, and the pooled confidence interval crossed zero.

## Supplementary Figure S2. Representative spatial expression maps

Representative Visium section showing PLEK, the frozen three-gene score, and the neutrophil module. The section was selected using the prespecified rule of the largest number of in-tissue spots. Maps are descriptive and do not constitute patient-level inference.
