# 论文推荐表述模板

## 中文标题建议

结直肠癌中血小板相关复合转录状态的跨队列解析揭示PLEK主导的髓系微环境关联

## English title

Cross-cohort dissection of a platelet-related composite transcriptional state reveals a PLEK-dominant myeloid microenvironment association in colorectal cancer

## 中文摘要结论

冻结PF4/PPBP/PLEK复合转录轴在多个CRC bulk转录组队列中稳定关联中性粒细胞相关炎症状态，但该关联主要由PLEK驱动。单细胞数据支持PLEK为中性粒细胞富集但非特异的髓系内源信号，而PPBP和PF4的稀有信号与ambient RNA或血小板–白细胞复合体相容。空间转录组仅提供弱、异质且非特异的支持。该状态不具有CRC总生存预测价值。以上结果表明，这一血小板相关复合信号更适合解释为具有异质来源的髓系微环境状态，而非肿瘤内血小板丰度或预后标志物。

## English abstract conclusion

The frozen PF4/PPBP/PLEK composite transcriptional axis was reproducibly associated with a neutrophil-related inflammatory state across colorectal cancer bulk transcriptomic cohorts, but this association was predominantly driven by PLEK. Single-cell data supported PLEK as an endogenous myeloid signal enriched, but not specific, to neutrophils, whereas the sparse PPBP and PF4 signals were compatible with ambient RNA or platelet–leukocyte complexes. Spatial transcriptomics provided only weak, heterogeneous, and non-specific support. The state was not associated with overall survival. Collectively, this platelet-related composite signal is better interpreted as a myeloid microenvironmental state with heterogeneous origins rather than a measure of intratumoral platelet abundance or a prognostic biomarker.

## Results核心句

- The frozen three-gene axis showed a reproducible positive association with the neutrophil module across five independent GEO cohorts.
- Gene-level analyses identified PLEK as the primary driver, PPBP as a stable secondary bulk component, and PF4 as a null component of this association.
- Single-cell analyses indicated that the donor-level association was primarily attributable to variation in PLEK-rich myeloid/neutrophil composition.
- Spatial associations were small, highly heterogeneous across patients, and not stronger for the neutrophil module than for the endothelial module.
- The frozen 13-gene state showed no overall-survival association and should not be interpreted as a prognostic signature.

## Discussion边界句

These observational transcriptomic findings do not establish direct platelet–neutrophil contact, cellular causality, or a platelet-specific origin of the composite signal.
