# Stage 16: three-gene-axis robustness and driver analysis

This locked analysis evaluates PF4, PPBP and PLEK against the fixed neutrophil module in five GEO CRC cohorts (1,055 samples).

It includes single-gene effects, every leave-one-gene-out score, rank/Z-score sensitivity, leave-one-cohort-out analysis, and 1,000 expression/variability-matched random three-gene modules.

The prespecified robustness criteria passed, but gene contributions were unequal: PLEK was the dominant driver, PPBP was a stable secondary component, and PF4 had no individual association. The result supports a robust composite score, not equal biological contribution by all three genes and not direct neutrophil transcription.
