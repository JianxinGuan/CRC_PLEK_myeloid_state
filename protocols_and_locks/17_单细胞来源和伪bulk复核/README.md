# Stage 17: single-cell source and donor pseudobulk review

The complete 3.79-million-cell CRC core atlas was read sparsely. The primary analysis retained 1,446,812 tumor-tissue cells from 381 donors, including 35,323 neutrophils from 221 donors. Donor x cell-type pseudobulk required at least 20 cells.

PLEK showed credible intracellular expression across myeloid cells and was most frequently detected in neutrophils, but it was not neutrophil-specific. PPBP and PF4 were rare in tumor neutrophils, study-dependent, and frequently compatible with ambient RNA or platelet-cell complexes. The frozen three-gene bulk association with neutrophil composition persisted after study adjustment and was primarily PLEK-driven.

The integrated atlas contains cell-associated counts but no empty-droplet matrix. Therefore SoupX/DecontX could not be validly run; ambient classifications are prespecified marker/count heuristics. All retained cells were labelled SOLO singlets, which does not exclude every platelet-leukocyte aggregate.
